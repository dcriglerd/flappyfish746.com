Data Deletion Request

Flappy Fish - Your Privacy Matters

We respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your Flappy Fish game data.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Uninstall the App

Simply uninstalling Flappy Fish from your device will remove all locally stored data including your game progress, settings, and preferences.

Option 2: Clear App Data

Go to your device Settings → Apps → Flappy Fish → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all app data without uninstalling.

Option 3: Email Request (for cloud data)

To delete your cloud-synced data (leaderboard scores, achievements), email us at:

flappyfishgame@gmail.com

Include your username (if you set one) in the email.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Game Progress: High scores, coins earned, games played
  • Unlocked Items: Fish skins, achievements
  • Username: Your display name on leaderboards
  • Streak Data: Daily login streaks and rewards
  • Device Identifier: Anonymous ID used for cloud sync
⚠️ Please Note:
  • Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • You will lose all progress, unlocked skins, and achievements
  • Your leaderboard ranking will be removed
  • Email requests are processed within 7 business days

Data Retention

If you don't request deletion, your data is retained to provide you with a seamless gaming experience across sessions and devices. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Questions?

For any questions about your data or this deletion process, please contact us at flappyfishgame@gmail.com
